# Code of Conduct

RebirthYourTV is a community-driven project dedicated to helping everyone experience an ultra-fast, clean, and ad-free TV experience. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow developers, contributors, and TV owners with courtesy and kindness. Many users are setting up their very first Android device or assisting elderly family members.
- **Provide Actionable Details:** When submitting issues or TV debloat lists, include specific details: device model, hardware chipset, firmware build, and Android OS version.
- **Privacy First:** NEVER post personal identifiers, private Wi-Fi names, IP addresses, serial numbers, Android IDs, or ADB keys in public issues or commits.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, insults, personal attacks, or aggressive behavior.
- Submitting lists or scripts designed to maliciously disable core system services or brick devices.
- Advertising commercial products, paid debloater services, or affiliate links.
