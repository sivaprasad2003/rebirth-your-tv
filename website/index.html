          EASY 5-MINUTE GUIDE
          
How It Works in 4 Steps

          
No cables needed. Connect over Wi-Fi, snapshot your TV, and experience true speed.

        


        

          

            
1

            
Enable ADB Debugging

            
On your TV: Settings → Device Preferences → About → Click "Build" 7 times. Then go to Developer Options and turn on USB Debugging.

          


          

            
2

            
Connect Over Wi-Fi

            
Connect from your computer using adb connect <TV_IP>:5555. Accept the "Allow USB debugging" prompt on your TV screen.

          


          

            
3

            
1-Click Full Backup

            
Run tools/backup.ps1 (or tools/backup.sh) to automatically record all 170+ packages and system properties into a safe timestamped folder.

          


          

            
4

            
Debloat & Launch

            
Run the debloater script and install RebirthYourTV launcher. Enjoy buttery-smooth 60 FPS and zero sponsored recommendations.
